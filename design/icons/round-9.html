Breathing room

The same grille, arch, dropped corners and red dot each time. What changes is how much of the tile the mark fills, how many dots it has, and the gap between them. "Margin" is the clear space from the mark to the tile's edge; "air" is the gap between two dots as a share of one dot's width.
